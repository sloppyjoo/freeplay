# Changelog

## FreePlay 0.1 beta

The first public release, for the Play+ 1.3.0 and the original Play 1.6.0 with an RT processor. Runs on my own Play+. The original Play build has only run on a virtual device.

**New**

- Slicer: pick a track with its select pad, set the slices, choose In time, Every step or My trigs, and apply. Trigs are made for you. Place cuts by hand with Points, or save each slice as its own sample.
- Two LFOs with seven shapes, tempo sync and restart on Play. Each LFO has its own amount for every track and parameter.
- Matrix: one knob per parameter, in a grid laid out like the knobs. The encoder works too.
- Hold Map and touch or turn a knob to map it.
- Knob pages show a parameter moving when an LFO is on it.
- Moving playhead in the waveform and envelope views.
- Sustain and Release on the Shift layer of the Attack / Decay knob.
- Sample Start / End editor with a waveform and zoom.
- MIDI CC presets: 8 slots of your own, and 43 synths.
- Knob-page visuals: Master VU meter and limiter history, Note keyboard, Volume and Pan meters, Filter curve, Attack / Decay envelope.
- Preset lists on Shift: reverb, delay, limiter, saturation, scale and root note.
- Folder colours in the sample pool and on the step pads.
- Tap tempo, Shift + encoder for bigger moves, Screen Brightness, Screen Dim, Knob Touch, File → Delete Project.
- Synth randomizer on the Play+.
- An installer that runs in your browser.

**Known limits**

- K66 Plays aren't supported yet.
- LFOs, Sustain and Release work on sample tracks only.
- Nothing has been tested on a real original Play.
